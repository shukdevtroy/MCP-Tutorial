## Smoky Cardamom Chicken
A weeknight favorite. The unusual part is combining warm cardamom with
smoked paprika — sounds odd, works great.

Ingredients:
- 500g chicken thighs
- 1 tsp ground cardamom
- 1.5 tsp smoked paprika
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp yogurt

Marinate for at least 2 hours, then pan-sear on medium-high until charred
at the edges.

## Notes
This dish came out best when I let the chicken marinate overnight instead
of just 2 hours.
